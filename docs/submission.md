# Submitting Picks

## Deadlines
- Always **one hour before the first game of the day** being picked.
- Sundays: **noon ET** (Sunday + Monday games are submitted together by then).
- Thursday games (weeks 1–17) may be submitted separately from Sunday/Monday picks.
- Wednesday games (Sept 9, Nov 25): deadline is one hour before that game; instructions come by email.
- Other days: instructions by email.

## Channels
| Channel | Rules |
|---|---|
| Email | Anytime before the deadline; include name, only the teams you pick to cover, and point values. Excel attachment OK. Must be time-stamped before deadline. Check email on Sunday for "not received" notices. |
| Phone | Game days only; Sunday 10:30 AM–noon ET. |
| Text | Thursday games **only**, never full sets. |
| US Mail | Not allowed. |

If the commissioners don't receive picks they notify you ASAP; if not fixed by Sunday 1:00 PM ET →
default (lowest) score and no weekly prize. Carp-ineligible players lose the courtesy notice.
For credit on a late-looking submission, forward the original email with its timestamp.

## Default lists
Two options; once added you **cannot opt off** for the season (joining mid-season is allowed).
Do not assume last year's default carries over.

1. **Standard default**: a fixed pick (e.g., "home team for 2 points") applied automatically to
   both Wednesday games and every Thursday game **except** Thanksgiving (week 13) and Christmas (week 17).
2. **Nuclear default**: defaults on **all** games not played Sunday at 1:00 PM ET or on Monday.

Providing a different pick than your default for a defaulted game → −10.

## Changes
Once submitted, picks cannot change unless fixing an error (to keep carp eligibility).

## Protests
Within two weeks of the score posting.

## Engagement
Before Monday night, check the bulletin board if you could score > 70 for the week; notify the
commissioners if the weekly winner is misidentified, or risk splitting the prize.
