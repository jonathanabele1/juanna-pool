# Overview

## Format
Weekly NFL pick'em against the spread (ATS) with confidence-point weighting. Each participant picks the
cover side of every game each week and assigns point values. Weekly scores accumulate into season
standings. Standings are posted by Wednesday each week (never before Wednesday) on the pool's
password-protected site, with the week's lines included.

## 2026 schedule notes
- Two Wednesday games: opener **Sept 9** and the pre-Thanksgiving game **Nov 25**.
- Six early Sunday games in Europe (weeks 4–7, 9, 10), plus one game in Australia and one in Mexico.
  These affect the "first game of the day" deadline (always one hour before the first game that day).
- Game days other than Thu/Sun/Mon (Wed, Fri, Sat, Tue, Christmas, Thanksgiving) get special
  instructions by email.
- Thursday games occur weeks 1–17. Thanksgiving = week 13, Christmas = week 17.
- Season length for weekly-prize math: **18 weeks** (see [prizes.md](prizes.md)).

## Entry fee
- **$95**, payable to the commissioner by cash, PayPal, or check.
- Participated **< 5 years** in the pool: due **before week 3**.
- Participated **≥ 5 years** (tenured): due **before week 7**.
- Unpaid by the applicable deadline → participation **voided**.

## Lines
- Source: vegasinsider.com NFL odds (Las Vegas), **bet365** lines (first column on the left).
- If no line appears Wednesday, the commissioners use another source.
- Picks must be listed in the order games appear on the site.
