# Picks and Scoring

## A weekly pick set
For every game that week, a participant provides:
1. The team they think **covers the spread**.
2. A **point value** (whole number) for that pick.

Picks are submitted in the order the games appear on the odds site.

## Point values
- Allowed values: integers **2 through 20**, plus **50** for the Lock of the Year (LOY).
- **Weekly total must be exactly 100** for full eligibility (the "carp" award).
- No values between 21 and 49 are ever allowed.

### Total over 100
| Excess over 100 | Penalty |
|---|---|
| 1–10 | −20 |
| 11–20 | −40 |
| 21–30 | −60 |
| ... (each further 10 over adds −20) | |

### Total under 100
- No point penalty, but **ineligible for the "carp" award**.

## Double-digit rule
- The number of games ranked 10 or higher must **not be exactly 4**. 0–3 or 5+ is fine.
- Exactly 4 double-digit games → **−20**.
- (Assumed: the 50-pt LOY counts as a double-digit game — see [open-questions.md](open-questions.md).)

## Lock of the Year (LOY)
- One per season, worth exactly **50** points, optional, single use.
- Once used it is gone; no other 21–49 value is ever valid.

## Missing games
- Each game with no pick: **−20**.
- The points available to distribute shrink by 20 per missed game: missing one game leaves 80 to
  distribute; missing all three Thanksgiving games leaves 40.
- A missed game also counts as a used double-digit slot for the "not exactly 4" rule
  (memo: missing Thursday means you can submit 1, 2, 4+ more double digits, totalling 80).
- Forgot one game but the rest sum to 100 → **−20** (the missing-game penalty).
- Submitted 15 of 16 and the sum is, e.g., 98: commissioners *may* correct; otherwise carp-ineligible.
- Missing the **entire weekend**: "a 10 or the lowest weekly score" (see open questions).
- Picks not received at all by Sunday 1:00 PM ET: ineligible for weekly prize and score = the
  week's **lowest score**.

## Weekly score
```
weekly_score = Σ(points of picks that covered)  −  penalties
```
Pushes: not addressed in the memo (see open questions).

## Weekly record vs spread
Count of correct picks vs incorrect picks for the week; used as the **only** weekly tiebreaker.
